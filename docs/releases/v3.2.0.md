# Crossvet 3.2.0: scores from your own use

Which model is good enough for the work you actually do? 3.2 lets a judge from another vendor score a sample of your real answers, checks that judge against your own ratings, and ranks models per kind of task.

## What's new
- **A sampled judge, opt-in.** `[score] enabled = true`, then `crossvet score run` (on a schedule). The judge comes from another vendor than the answer, scores 1 to 5 against a rubric for the kind of task, and has its own daily cap. Private-looking requests are never judged; `[score] apps` limits which apps are scored.
- **Shadow answers.** A cheaper model answers some of the same questions in the background and is judged the same way, showing where it would have been good enough.
- **Calibration.** `crossvet score calibrate` reports how often the judge agrees with your `/good` and `/bad`; a judge that disagrees too often is flagged and kept out of routing.
- **Leaderboard.** `crossvet leaderboard` (and the MCP tool) shows score, a cautious score and cost per answer for each model and kind of task. Judge scores sync to Langfuse.

## Upgrade
`uv tool install --force "git+https://github.com/j-zulick/crossvet@v3.2.0"`. Nothing changes until you turn scoring on.
