# Crossvet v1.10.0

v1.10 widens the injection check and adds hidden-text channels to `crossvet-scan`. Scan reports are quieter on ordinary pages, and the package is ready for PyPI.

## What's new

### Wider patterns
The injection check now covers:
- Italian and Dutch;
- role changes, commands about the reply, and instructions to omit something, in all six non-English languages;
- commands about the reader's own reply in English ("add … to your response").

### Disguises undone before matching
- Look-alike letters, invisible characters, letter-spaced words, ROT13 and reversed text are undone before matching.
- An instruction split into short fragments is joined and reported as `split:<kind>`.
- Paraphrased instructions are still missed.

### New hidden-text channels in `crossvet-scan`
- Text hidden by the page's own `<style>` rules.
- `<template>` content, SVG titles and descriptions, and hidden form inputs.
- JSON-LD and noscript.
- More ways of hiding text inline: off-screen, clipped, scaled to nothing, transparent and near-zero size.
- Text split across inline elements is read as one run.

### Quieter scan reports
- A channel whose only finding is a repeated claim is left out unless you pass `--include-repetition`.
- On 608 real pages, `crossvet-scan` flagged 24 with v1.9 and 1 with v1.10. 23 of the 24 v1.9 findings were menus and templates. The one page left is a discussion that addresses AI models.
- The flag the research pipeline writes into its notes is unchanged on those pages: 6 before, 6 after.
- Details: `docs/benchmarks/2026-11-19-scan-patterns/`. Design decision 25.

### Fixed: a stall on very long pages
- The repetition check could run for more than 20 seconds on a page with millions of characters of text.
- It now judges a page on its first 20,000 words and skips sentences that can't recur often enough. A test covers the case.

### Ready for PyPI
- Package metadata is complete.
- The wheel no longer installs a top-level `tests` package.
- pytest and ipykernel moved to a dev dependency group.
- A release workflow publishes to PyPI with trusted publishing when a GitHub release is published.

## Upgrading
- `crossvet-scan` and the `scan_for_injection` MCP tool no longer report repetition-only findings. Pass `--include-repetition` to get them back.
- Pages that disguise an instruction now produce new kinds in reports and research notes: `obfuscated:…`, `rot13:…`, `reversed:…` and `split:…`.
- When developing, `uv sync` installs the dev group (pytest) as before.
