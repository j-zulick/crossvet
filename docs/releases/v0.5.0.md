# Crossvet v0.5.0

Reviewers can now argue before they vote, and nobody is allowed to back down without evidence.

## Highlights

- **Deliberation rounds** (`REVIEW_DELIBERATION_ROUNDS=1..3`, off by default). After the critic, each critique gets a challenger: a different reviewer, rotating every round, who contests specific findings and can quote the research notes. The author then maintains, revises or withdraws each contested finding.
- **Sycophancy guard.** A revision or withdrawal is applied only when a quote from the challenger or the author is found verbatim in the research notes. The check runs in code, not in a model. Any other change of position is ignored, the original finding stays, and the change is flagged as possible sycophancy in the Review notes.
- **Bounded cost.** At most three rounds; rounds stop as soon as no challenger contests anything; a failed call skips that exchange instead of failing the run.
- **Measured, not assumed.** `crossvet-compare` now reports grounded and ungrounded position changes. `examples/compare/deliberation.toml` runs the same panel with and without deliberation so you can see whether it improves your reports before turning it on.

## Cost

Each round adds up to two calls per critique (challenge and response).
