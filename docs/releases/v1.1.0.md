# Crossvet v1.1.0

Deliberation that stops when it stops helping.

## New in 1.1

- **Explicit engagement.** In every deliberation round the challenger labels each finding AGREE, DISAGREE or BUILD_ON, by its anonymous id. Disagreements go to the author; additions count only with a passage from the research notes, checked in code.
- **Early stopping decided in code.** Deliberation ends on consensus (no finding moved and the challengers endorsed enough of them) or on a stable disagreement (nothing moved for two rounds), instead of always running to the cap.
- **A guard against false consensus.** Agreement that cites nothing in the notes, or a silent challenger, earns one more round with the next challenger before deliberation may stop.
- **Measured, not assumed.** The Review notes say why deliberation stopped and how many rounds it saved. The same numbers go to the run export, `crossvet-compare` and Langfuse, and `crossvet-setup --compare-plan` writes a fixed vs early-stop pair to measure the saving on your own questions.

## Upgrading

Early stopping is on by default when deliberation is on (`REVIEW_DELIBERATION_ROUNDS` > 0). `REVIEW_DELIBERATION_EARLY_STOP=false` restores the 1.0 behaviour. New settings: `REVIEW_CONSENSUS_THRESHOLD`, `REVIEW_DELIBERATION_MIN_ROUNDS`, `REVIEW_CONSENSUS_MIN_GROUNDED_SHARE`.
