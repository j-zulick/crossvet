# Crossvet v0.7.0

Turn your own research runs into training data for a model on your Mac.

## Highlights

- **`crossvet-dataset`** converts run exports into the chat format `mlx_lm.lora` reads, written as `train.jsonl`, `valid.jsonl` and `test.jsonl`.
- **Two tasks.**
  - `critique` teaches a local model to review like the hosted panel: the target is the critique the panel voted most useful, after the critic and any deliberation removed findings the notes contradict.
  - `revise` teaches report revision: the target is a revision that succeeded and cites only URLs the research actually read.
- **No leakage.** Splits are by research brief, so a question never appears in both training and test data. Duplicates and malformed lines are skipped, and every skipped run is counted with a reason.
- **Richer exports.** Run export `schema_version` 2 adds the research notes, critique labels and revision feedback. Runs exported by earlier versions are skipped with an explanation.

## Measuring before and after

Train and fuse on a Mac with `mlx_lm.lora` and `mlx_lm.fuse`, serve the fused model with `mlx_lm.server`, then run `crossvet-compare` with the base and fine-tuned model as a `local:` reviewer.

## Notes

Exports contain text fetched from the web. Check your sources' terms before training on them and keep datasets private. Training itself needs Apple silicon and is not run in CI.
