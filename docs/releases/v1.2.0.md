# Crossvet v1.2.0

Reviewers decide what a good answer needs before they read the draft.

## New in 1.2

- **Blind first pass.** With `REVIEW_BLIND_PASS_ENABLED=true`, every reviewer outlines the answer from the research notes alone (up to 6 points, each with its supporting passage) before it sees the draft.
- **Omissions checked in code.** Points whose passage is not in the notes are dropped. Grounded points the draft does not cover are added to that reviewer's critique, so they reach the vote and the reviser. Reviewers also see their own outline while they critique.
- **Measured.** The Review notes report points, coverage and omissions; the export keeps every outline; `crossvet-compare` and Langfuse record `blind_omissions`, and the compare plan adds a `blind-pass` setup.

## Limits

Coverage is a word-overlap check, so a correct paraphrase can be flagged as an omission. Flags go to the reviser as feedback, not into the report.
